How to drive

On a phone or tablet

◀
▶
BRAKE
GAS
  • Hold GAS to go and BRAKE to stop.
  • Steer with the arrows, or press Tilt and turn the phone like a wheel.
  • View changes the camera. ✕ leaves.

With a keyboard

  • W A S D or the ↑ ← ↓ → keys: gas, steer, brake.
  • C changes the camera, H is the handbrake.
  • A gamepad works too. The gearbox is automatic.

This is a short preview of neoAC, a free, open-source rebuild of Assetto Corsa's engine. It looks simpler than the desktop version and has no sound.

The car and the track are free community mods from assettocorsa.club, shown with thanks to their authors: Mercedes-Benz 300 SEL 6.3 (physics by Dmitrii A) and Nevada Drift. More are on the cars and tracks pages. All rights belong to their authors.
